❔ HOW TO PLAY

  • 🕹️ Steer with A/D or ←/→ — move freely across the 3-lane highway.

  • 💎 Collect glowing cyan orbs for score & a small Nitro refill.
  • ⚠️ Dodge laser gates, drone mines & barricades — crashing costs a life and resets your Nitro.
  • 📈 The highway never ends and keeps accelerating — survive as long as you can!
