🏹 ARCHERY DUEL

Draw • Aim • Outlast the Storm

RIVAL DIFFICULTY

Desktop: Drag to draw & aim the bow · Shift / Right-click hold breath
Mobile: Touch & drag to draw, release to fire · tap HOLD BREATH

❔ HOW TO PLAY

  • 🏹 Press and drag on the screen to draw your bow. The drag direction aims the shot and the drag distance sets power.
  • 🌬️ Watch the wind compass — crosswinds push your arrow sideways, headwinds slow it, tailwinds help it fly.
  • 🫁 Hold Shift or Right-click (or the mobile button) to hold your breath and steady the reticle's natural drift.
  • 🎯 Each round you and your rival shoot 3 arrows at a target 30–100m away. Closer to the gold center scores more (10 down to 1).
  • ⛈️ Weather changes every round: clear sunset, thunderstorm, blizzard, or sandstorm — each bends your arrow differently.
  • 🏆 Highest total score after all rounds wins the duel!
