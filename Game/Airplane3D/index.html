❔ HOW TO PLAY

  • 🕹️ Pitch with W/S or ↑/↓, roll with A/D or ←/→, yaw with Q/E.

  • 🔫 Fire with Space or mouse click — take down enemy drones for points.
  • ⚠️ Don't fly into the ground or hills — crashing costs heavy damage!
  • 🌍 The world is infinite — terrain, rivers, trees and clouds generate forever as you fly.
